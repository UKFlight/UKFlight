About UKFlight

What is UKFlight?

UKFlight is a free flight simulator built to bring authentic flight simulation to PC systems of all specs, with a focus on the United Kingdom and Ireland regions.

Why UKFlight?

Most flight simulators require high-end hardware or significant financial investment. UKFlight changes that. We believe everyone should have access to a quality flight sim experience, regardless of their computer's specs.

Key Features

  • Runs on minimal specs — low RAM and storage requirements
  • Authentic UK and Ireland airspace and airports
  • Realistic flight physics and aircraft handling
  • Multiple authentic aircraft models
  • Completely free, no paywalls or DLC

Our Vision

We're building UKFlight to be the go-to flight simulator for pilots and enthusiasts who want a genuine flying experience without the barriers to entry. Updates will continue to add new aircraft, airports, and features as the project grows.
